Terms and Community rules

HiStreets · Updated 19 October 2026

HiStreets is operated by Ahtashamul Haque. It helps residents discover Newham businesses, offers, jobs and community activities. Contact: ahaque@atomicmail.io.

Using HiStreets

You can browse without an account and choose whether to share your location. Business accounts must provide accurate information and have permission to represent their organisation. Keep passwords and sign-in codes private.

Community rules

You must accept these rules before submitting a post. You are responsible for the information and files you submit and must have the right to share them.

  • No fraud, scams, spam, impersonation or misleading claims.
  • No unlawful offers, dangerous instructions, threats, harassment, hate or discriminatory content.
  • No sexually explicit content, exploitation, child abuse or material that puts children at risk.
  • No private information or images of another person without permission.
  • Jobs, offers and community activities must be genuine and include accurate terms. Do not invent prices, qualifications, pay or availability.

Reporting and moderation

Use Report on a post, business listing or AI response to send a concern to HiStreets. Reports are reviewed by the operator or authorised administrators. Content may be rejected or removed and accounts restricted for rule violations. For urgent support, email the contact above; HiStreets is not an emergency service.

Block business hides that business and its posts on your current device. Manage blocks under Business → Privacy & safety. Clearing app data removes local blocks. Blocking is separate from reporting and does not itself remove content for everyone.

Listings and applications

Public directory listings may be unclaimed or unverified. Check details directly with the business. Approved accounts can submit posts; automated checks may publish eligible posts and hold others for review. Employers receive applications and make recruitment decisions; HiStreets does not guarantee an offer, vacancy, job or service.

AI assistance

Ask HiStreets and Business Copilot may use Groq. Do not enter confidential or sensitive personal information. AI output can be inaccurate and must be checked. Business Copilot drafts cannot publish without your action. Use Report AI response to flag problems.

External services and availability

Maps, directions, business websites and messaging links may use external providers with their own terms. Parking is not yet available; always check official signs. Service availability and listing accuracy are not guaranteed. The current Android app has no advertisements, subscriptions or purchases.

Account and data deletion

Use Delete account or data to request deletion without reinstalling the app. Read our Privacy Policy for how personal information is handled.

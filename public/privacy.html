HiStreets Privacy Policy

HiStreets is a local platform for Newham. We collect only the information needed to run business accounts, business posts, job applications, verification and moderation.

What we collect

For businesses, we collect login email, business details, public posts, verification status and any optional verification photos submitted during registration. For job applicants, we collect the contact details, note and CV they choose to submit for a job application. For visitors, location is optional and is used to show nearby results.

Location

If you allow location, your browser shares it with the app so nearby offers, jobs and local places can be shown. HiStreets does not use location for continuous tracking.

Why we use it

We use this information to show local offers, jobs and community posts, manage business access, verify businesses, moderate content and deliver job applications to the relevant business.

Verification evidence

Business verification photos are private and available only to authorised admins for the verification decision. They are not shown on public listings. The admin workflow removes submitted verification photos after the business is approved or rejected and clears the written verification note, while keeping the basic verification result and audit record.

Job applications and CVs

CV files are stored privately. The relevant business and authorised admins can open a temporary secure link to a CV. CVs are not published as public files.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising trackers. We do not use personal data for unrelated marketing.

Where it is stored

Service data is stored using Supabase with the HiStreets deployment configured for UK/EU hosting.

Your rights

You can contact the HiStreets operator to request access, correction or deletion of personal data. Signed-in business users can also use the account controls available in the Business area.

Contact

Contact the HiStreets operator for privacy requests.

Privacy Policy

HiStreets · Updated 19 October 2026

This policy covers the HiStreets web application and Android app. The operator is Ahtashamul Haque, contactable at ahaque@atomicmail.io. The separate marketing website may offer optional Google Analytics cookies; the Android app does not include Google Analytics or advertising SDKs.

Information used to provide the service

  • Accounts and businesses: login email, authentication records, business profile/contact information, ownership requests, membership invitations, public posts and optional uploaded business images or verification evidence.
  • Job applications: name, email, phone, covering note and any CV supplied. The relevant business and authorised administrators can access these privately; they are not published as directory content.
  • Reports and support: report reason, details and a copy of the reported content. A signed-in reporter's account identifier may be associated with the report. Report text is available to authorised moderators, not other visitors.
  • Activity and security: service providers process network addresses and technical logs. HiStreets records business interactions such as listing views, directions clicks and application starts for aggregate dashboards. Rate limits use derived network or account identifiers.

Location and maps

Location access is optional, requested when you choose to find nearby places, and limited to foreground use. Approximate location is supported. HiStreets does not run continuous background location tracking. Coordinates help sort nearby results and centre the map. Loading map tiles can reveal the viewed area to the map provider; opening directions passes the selected destination to Google Maps. You can browse Newham without location permission and revoke permission in Android settings.

AI requests

Ask HiStreets sends the text you enter to our backend. Requests may be processed by Google Gemini to interpret a search; Business Copilot may send your instructions and relevant business details to Gemini to draft a post. Do not enter passwords, private documents or sensitive personal information. Some requests are handled by local rules on the server, but automatic detection is not a guarantee that every sensitive request stays away from external AI.

Selected commercial searches may contribute to aggregate area/category demand signals. These signals do not contain the full search text. When you report an AI response, the displayed response and the details you choose to enter are sent to moderators; your original question is not attached automatically.

Providers and sharing

Supabase provides database, authentication, file storage and server functions; the project is configured in the EU (Frankfurt). Map tiles use OpenFreeMap and postcode lookups use Postcodes.io. Optional AI processing uses Google Gemini. Operational providers may process information beyond the database's hosting region. Public posts and business information are visible to visitors. Job applications are shared with the business you apply to. External links, including Google Maps and WhatsApp, open the selected provider's service.

Purpose and protection

Information is used to provide accounts and listings, process applications, support owners, prevent misuse, moderate content and understand service usage. Connections use HTTPS. Access controls restrict private files; authorised users open temporary signed links. We do not sell personal data or use it for unrelated marketing.

Storage and deletion

Data is kept while needed for the service and reviewed when you request deletion. Verification evidence is intended to be removed after a verification decision; failed cleanup requires administrator follow-up. Public posts may expire while their underlying records remain until removed. Reports remain available for moderation until resolved and reviewed for removal. We do not promise that uninstalling the app deletes server records.

Request permanent account and associated data deletion, or request deletion of an application/CV without an account. Requests are checked and processed manually. We will explain any specific legal or security retention requirement rather than treating account deactivation as deletion. Provider backups and copies held by recipients may have separate deletion cycles.

On your device

The app stores authentication sessions and local preferences, including blocked businesses. Android backups are disabled for the app. Sign out to remove the local session; use the deletion page to remove server-held account data. Blocks are specific to the device and disappear if you clear app data.

Questions and rights

Contact ahaque@atomicmail.io to ask about your data or request access, correction or deletion. Please identify your account or submission without sending your password.

Delete your HiStreets account or data

HiStreets · Updated 26 October 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your HiStreets account and associated personal data here. You do not need to install the app or sign in.

Request deletion in HiStreets

Sign in, open the Business tab and find Privacy & safety. Choose Delete account and associated data, review the information and confirm your request. No separate support email is needed. We aim to complete deletion within 30 days and will email you when it is complete.

Open HiStreets to request deletion

Help if you cannot sign in, or want to delete other data

Email ahaque@atomicmail.io with the subject HiStreets account and data deletion. Use your registered email where possible and tell us that you want your account and associated data deleted. Do not send your password or identity documents.

Request account deletion by email

If this button does not open an email app, copy the address into your email service and send the request there. Sending the email submits a request; opening this page does not delete your account.

What happens next

The HiStreets operator, Ahtashamul Haque, checks that the request comes from the account holder, confirms the scope and processes it manually. If you cannot access your registered email, contact the same address for help. You will receive confirmation when deletion is complete, or an explanation of any information that must be retained and why.

What deletion covers

The request covers your login account, profile, personal contact details, your submitted verification files and notes, and personal data in your posts, membership and support records. We also review uploaded files and provider-held data associated with the account. Shared business records and other people's job applications must be handled separately so that their data and access are protected. Independent public directory information about a business may remain; your personal account details and ownership link are removed.

If information must be retained for a legal or security obligation, we will explain the specific reason and retention period. Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete stored account data.

Job applications or other data without an account

You can use the same email address to request deletion of a CV, job application or report. Include the email used and enough information to identify the submission. An employer may hold a separate copy of an application you sent them; we can help identify the relevant business.

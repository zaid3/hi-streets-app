HiStreets Terms

HiStreets is a local platform for Newham. By using it, you agree to use it lawfully and respectfully.

Public use

Visitors can browse the map, offers, jobs and community posts without signing in. Location is optional and is used to show nearby results.

Business access

Businesses use the secure Business area to register or request ownership, complete public details and submit posts. A business must be approved before it appears publicly. Verification evidence must relate to the business being submitted and must not contain unnecessary sensitive personal documents.

Jobs

Job applicants can apply without signing in. A CV is required. The relevant business is responsible for recruitment decisions and contacting applicants.

Posts

Offers, jobs, free meals and community posts must be genuine, lawful and safe. Misleading, fraudulent, discriminatory, illegal or unsafe content is not allowed. Posts that pass the platform checks may publish automatically; others can be held for review or removed.

Reviews

Public user reviews are not part of the current HiStreets service.

Parking

Parking is coming soon. Do not rely on HiStreets for parking rules until verified parking information is added. Always check roadside signs, bay plates and road markings before parking.

Service availability

HiStreets may update, correct or remove listings and posts to keep the local service accurate and safe. External links such as directions and business websites are provided for convenience and are operated by third parties.

No monetisation

The current HiStreets service has no ads, payments or tracking SDKs.
